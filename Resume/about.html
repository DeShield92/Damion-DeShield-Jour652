More About Me


My professional background sits at the intersection of business operations, information management, and technology. Through experience in higher education, public service, policy research, libraries, and multimedia production, I have developed a strong foundation in coordinating projects, analyzing information, improving processes, and supporting organizational needs. I am interested in using this combination of skills to help organizations operate more efficiently and make informed, technology-driven decisions.

I currently work as a Coordinator at the University of Maryland’s Robert H. Smith School of Business, where I support graduate admissions operations, coordinate administrative processes, manage competing priorities, and work with data to inform decisions. This role has strengthened my ability to identify operational needs, communicate with stakeholders, and support the systems and workflows that enable an organization to function effectively.

My previous experience in federal advocacy, public libraries, and archival research has further developed my analytical, organizational, and communication skills. At the National League of Cities, I researched congressional committees and members, maintained information used for advocacy initiatives, and contributed digital content. My library and archival work also strengthened my ability to evaluate sources, organize complex information, maintain accurate records, and make information accessible to different audiences.

I am building on this experience through continued technical and professional development. I hold a CompTIA Tech+ certification and am expanding my knowledge of HTML, CSS, digital tools, multimedia workflows, and process improvement. Through my Graduate Certificate in Multimedia Journalism at the University of Maryland, I am developing additional skills in digital communication, social media analytics, multimedia production, and media entrepreneurship.

My primary career interests are Business Analysis and IT Coordination, particularly roles that connect business needs with technology solutions, improve workflows, support applications and systems, and help teams use information effectively. Over the longer term, I am also interested in Cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC), where I can build on my background in research, documentation, information management, and operational coordination to support risk-aware decision-making, policy implementation, and responsible technology use.

I bring a multidisciplinary perspective, a commitment to continuous learning, and an interest in bridging people, processes, and technology. My goal is to help organizations solve operational challenges, improve business outcomes, and develop reliable, well-managed technology environments.
